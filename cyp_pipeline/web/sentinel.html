How to read this screen

IsoForm compares supported prepared pockets and helps plan counter-screening. It does not automatically prove binding to a new target. UMA energies and derived proxies guide research; experimental selectivity needs paired assays.

Structure lines are coordinate-based visual guides, not predictions of binding interactions. New target–drug pairs require pose generation and reviewed chemistry before UMA.
